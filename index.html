Module 1Computer Networking for Cloud, DevOps & AI Systems

The networking foundation required for cloud platforms, distributed systems and AI-driven applications.

  • Introduction to NetworkingEvolution of networking and its role in cloud and AI platforms · Requirements for distributed and AI workloads
  • Networking Devices & ArchitectureRouters, switches, firewalls, load balancers · Role of networking devices in cloud-native and AI architectures

  • Networking for Cloud & AI WorkloadsVPC networking basics · Low-latency communication for data-intensive applications

  • Linux Fundamentals & ArchitectureLinux evolution and its importance in cloud and AI environments

  • Linux Networking & SecuritySSH, firewall basics, network troubleshooting
  • Shell Scripting & AutomationAutomating system tasks · Preparing systems for AI-enabled DevOps workflows

Design scalable, secure cloud architectures that support AI and data-driven workloads.

  • Cloud Computing FundamentalsIaaS, PaaS, SaaS · Elasticity, scalability, high availability

  • Container & Kubernetes ServicesEKS, ECS, ECR
